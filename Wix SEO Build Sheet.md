# PrimeTurf — Wix SEO build sheet

**What this is for:** getting Google-ready structure onto the new Wix site before it replaces primeturf.co.za. Design and photos can come later; the items below decide whether you keep your current rankings.

Work top to bottom. Everything is done in the Wix editor (not Harmony) unless it says otherwise.

---

## 0. Critical: the domain switch (do this LAST)

primeturf.co.za currently points to your **old** Wix site, and that is where Google's rankings live. The new site (primeturf4) only inherits them if:
1. every slug below is set **before** the switch,
2. the redirects in section 3 are imported into the **new** site,
3. then, in the old site: Settings → Domains → disconnect primeturf.co.za; in the new site: Connect Domain → primeturf.co.za.

Don't delete the old site for at least 3 months — keep it as a backup.

---

## 1. Fix now: soft vs hard installation is wrong on Home

Harmony swapped the definitions and put prices on them. The live site says the opposite. Replace the two cards with:

- **Hard installation**: artificial turf bonded directly onto an existing hard surface such as paving, concrete, tiles or pool surrounds. Minimal preparation: we clean the surface and apply premium adhesives.
- **Soft installation**: artificial turf installed on natural ground or soil, using our 7-step process: weed prevention, precision levelling, drainage, a stable base, compaction, secure bonding and precision installation.

Remove the "Standard / Premium" titles, the prices and the "View Pricing" buttons from these two cards. Prices belong only in the pricing section.

## 7. Google Search Console (after the domain is connected)

1. Wix Dashboard → **Marketing & SEO → SEO → Get found on Google** → connect Google Search Console (Wix verifies automatically).
2. Wix submits `/sitemap.xml` automatically. Confirm it shows in Search Console → Sitemaps.
3. In Search Console → **URL Inspection**, request indexing for: Home, Pricing post, /artificial-grass-products, /artificial-turf-gauteng, /artificial-grass-cape-town, /artificial-grass-sandton, /artificial-grass-pretoria-east.
4. Check **Pages → Not indexed** a week later. Old .html URLs should show "Page with redirect", which is correct.
