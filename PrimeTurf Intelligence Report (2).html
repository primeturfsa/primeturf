4. Market intelligence

Pricing

Competitors quote roughly R350 to R850/m² installed in Cape Town PUBLIC CLAIM LOW. PrimeTurf's site quotes R200 to R850/m² by range. The low end sits below the market, so a mystery-shop comparison of 3 to 5 quotes is recommended before changing anything.

Competitor channel moves

Arturf Africa, SynGrass, Synthetic Lawn and Local Pros court installers and landscapers with trade pricing or vetted networks. MightyGrass is exhibiting at Big 5 Construct SA 2026. A PrimeTurf partner offer would need to compete on warranty, install quality and lead flow, not price.

Tenders

No open 2026 artificial-turf tender found in Gauteng or the Western Cape across all runs. Stellenbosch BSM 88/24 (R23,085, to 30 Jun 2027) is already awarded; the next round is the entry point. Public web search indexes tenders poorly, so set TenderFlow keyword alerts and check etenders.gov.za by hand.

Sports and schools

School hockey and sports pitches are dominated by specialists (Belgotex Sport, Synsport and others). Treat as a poor fit unless PrimeTurf holds sports-grade product. Padel is similar and needs a product-fit check. Decision for Stiaan.

Hospitality watchlist

No turf scope or named landscaper for any of these: The Granger (Green Point, R2bn, hotel and apartments), Cape Town EDITION (rooftop pool), Waterfall City hotel (Midrand, R750m), NH Johannesburg Sandton refurbishment. Create a company record only when outdoor works and a decision-maker appear.

Rejected as stale or noise

Evergreen Lifestyle (c. 2021), Helderfontein (2022), Redwood Estate (Jan 2025), Newinbosch (2022), Beachwood (KZN), Eastway Midstream (opening Oct 2026, scope likely already contracted), Drakenzicht Paarl (status conflicting), Hudson Park astroturf (Eastern Cape, already awarded).
