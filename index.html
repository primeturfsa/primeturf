Installation types

What's the difference between a soft and a hard installation?

1

Hard installation

Artificial turf professionally bonded directly onto an existing hard surface — paving, concrete, tiles or pool surrounds.

Because the base is already in place, minimal preparation is needed. We clean and prepare the surface thoroughly, then apply premium adhesives for a neat, secure and long-lasting finish.

2

Soft installation

Artificial turf installed on natural ground or soil, using our professional 7-step installation process to build a durable foundation.

  • 1 · Weed prevention
  • 2 · Precision levelling
  • 3 · Proper drainage
  • 4 · Structurally stable base
  • 5 · Professional compaction
  • 6 · Secure bonding
  • 7 · Precision installation

Common questions

How much does artificial grass cost?

Artificial grass installation ranges from R365–R865 per square metre depending on turf grade and site requirements. A standard 80m² residential garden typically costs R29,200–R69,200 fully installed. Contact PrimeTurf for a precise quote for your specific property.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations take 1–2 days. Complex projects with extensive ground preparation or large surface areas may take 3–5 days. We work efficiently to minimise disruption to your household.

Is artificial grass allowed in estates?

Many estates and body corporates allow artificial grass, but rules differ. We recommend checking with your HOA first. PrimeTurf can provide specification documents and product information to assist with estate approvals.

What warranty do you provide?

Each turf range carries its own manufacturer guarantee — 5 years on Deco and 6 years across the residential and estate ranges — plus a 24-month PrimeTurf workmanship guarantee. Both are issued in writing at handover.
