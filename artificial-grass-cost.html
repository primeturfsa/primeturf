What moves the number

Six things that decide your rate

Turf grade and pile height

The single biggest lever. A 20mm decorative turf and a 35mm estate turf are different products at different prices — compare the range.

How much ground work the site needs

Laying onto sound existing paving is quick. Lifting an old lawn, excavating clay and building a compacted stone base is the expensive part — and the part that decides whether the lawn still looks level in five years.

Area and shape

Bigger is cheaper per m². Awkward shapes with lots of curves and cut-outs waste turf off the roll, which pushes the rate back up.

Access

If rolls and rubble have to go through a house or up stairs instead of through a side gate, that is labour, and it shows in the quote.

Drainage

Most gardens drain fine through the sub-base. Low-lying areas, clay and Western Cape winter rainfall sometimes need additional drainage laid in.

Extras that aren't turf

Putting-green contouring, painted play markings, artificial hedging, new edging or paving — all quoted as separate line items so you can see what you are paying for.

Pricing questions

How much does artificial grass cost per square metre in South Africa?

Fully installed, R365–R865 per m² in 2026: Standard R365–R465, Premium R515–R665, Luxury R715–R865. Turf grade, site preparation and location decide where you land. These are indicative ranges — we fix the price in writing after a free site measure.

What does the installed price include?

Site clearing, the compacted sub-base and drainage layer, weed membrane, the turf, seaming, edging, infill, brushing and rubble removal. Paving, retaining, irrigation removal and lighting are quoted separately and itemised.

Why is the rate cheaper on a bigger lawn?

Travel, machine hire, set-up and offcut waste are largely fixed. A 30m² courtyard carries the same set-up as a 200m² lawn, so the rate per m² on a small area is always higher.

Is it cheaper than a natural lawn over time?

The installation is a single up-front cost, after which there is no watering, mowing, fertiliser or re-seeding. Whether that pays back faster than a natural lawn depends on your water tariff, the area and what you currently spend on garden services — worth working out on your own numbers rather than taking a supplier's word for it.

Do you charge for a quote or site visit?

No. The site assessment and the written quote are free, with no obligation.
